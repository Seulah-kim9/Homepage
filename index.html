What the wind lifts,
and what it moves.

Dust: I model where it comes from with WRF-Chem, and read 28 years of satellite and ground observations to see how East Asian source regions are changing.

Flight: I model how an air vehicle responds to city wind, and ask how much turbulence between buildings can push it off course.
